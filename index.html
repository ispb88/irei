Indianapolis Real Estate Investing Resources

Associations #

  • CIREIA (Central Indiana Real Estate Investors Association)
    Indiana's largest investor club and a nonprofit. Monthly general meeting on the second Tuesday, REI 101 workshops for new investors, legal and legislative updates, and specialty series such as Dinner and Deals with Dave Short.
  • MIBOR MLS
    The professional association and MLS for real estate agents in Central Indiana.

  • Berkshire Law
    Jynell Berkshire's near-eastside firm. She's also a broker and longtime investor. Handles leases, investor contracts, landlord-tenant disputes, entity formation, quiet title and tax sale challenges, plus code enforcement and zoning issues with the city. Most work is fixed-fee.
  • Jeff Jinks Law
    North side real estate, business and estate planning firm founded in 1993. Covers due diligence, entity setup, 1031 exchanges and litigation for investors. Jeff is a frequent CIREIA speaker and has been the attorney and lobbyist for the Indiana State REIA.
  • McAnlis Law Group
    Joanne McAnlis' northeast side real estate firm. She's also a broker and investor with 25+ years of experience, and advises several local property management companies. Handles evictions, damage claims and judgment recovery for landlords, plus purchases, leases, entity formation and real estate license defense.
  • Rollins Law Group
    Chad Rollins' Fishers firm. Has handled hundreds of evictions for landlords in Marion, Hamilton and nearby counties, and advises out-of-state and turnkey investors on purchases and property managers on their contracts.

CPAs & Accountants #

  • Barnes Dennig
    Regional CPA firm with offices on the east and west sides of Indianapolis. Its real estate group handles tax planning, cost segregation studies, 1031 exchanges and entity selection for commercial real estate owners and investment partnerships.
  • Busch Tax Company
    Nate Busch's CPA firm, based in New Albany, Ohio, and founded in 2016. A popular choice among Indianapolis investors. Handles tax planning and preparation, bookkeeping, payroll and IRS audit representation.
  • Katz, Sapper & Miller (KSM)
    Indianapolis-based accounting firm founded more than 80 years ago, with 130+ real estate professionals. Best suited to larger investors, developers and syndicators: cost segregation, like-kind exchanges, Opportunity Zones, tax increment financing and fund structuring.

Government #

  • Indianapolis Historic Preservation Commission
    Reviews exterior work in local historic districts such as Old Northside, Lockerbie Square and Irvington. Rehabs, demolition and new construction there need a Certificate of Appropriateness before work starts, so check before you buy.
  • Indianapolis Housing Agency Landlord Resources
    How to rent to Section 8 (Housing Choice Voucher) tenants in Marion County. Units must pass an IHA inspection, rents are capped at IHA limits, and IHA pays its share directly to the landlord.
  • Indianapolis Sheriff Sale
    Monthly mortgage foreclosure auctions held online through GovEase. Register at least a week ahead. Properties sell as-is with no interior access and may carry prior liens, so do your own title search.
  • Indianapolis Tax Sale
    Annual Marion County tax sale held online through GovEase each fall. You bid on tax sale certificates, not deeds. If the owner doesn't pay back the taxes within the redemption period, you can petition for the deed.
  • Indy Accela Citizen Access
    City portal for permits and code enforcement cases. Search by address without an account to check permit history and open violations before you buy. An account lets you pull permits and register as a landlord.
  • Marion County Assessor Property Cards
    Look up any parcel by address, owner or parcel number. Cards show assessed values, building details, lot size and sale history.
  • MyCase (Indiana Courts)
    Free statewide court case search by name or case number. Check for foreclosures, evictions, liens and lawsuits involving a seller, tenant or property.
  • Vacant to Vibrant (Indianapolis Land Bank)
    City land bank run by the Dept. of Metropolitan Development. Sells vacant lots and abandoned houses, mostly leftovers from the tax sale. Buyers apply monthly and most sales require a two-year agreement to rehab or build.

Home Inspectors #

  • AllCheck Inspections
    Indianapolis inspection company covering Central Indiana, with hundreds of Google reviews. Adds radon, sewer scope, termite, mold and water testing to a standard inspection, plus light commercial inspections. Reports are delivered within 24 hours.
  • Every Nook and Cranny Home Inspection (Wylie Womack)
    InterNACHI-certified inspector covering Marion and surrounding counties, recommended by local investors. Add-ons include radon, sewer line, mold, termite and thermal imaging. Offers weekend appointments, and you can schedule online and walk through with him.
  • Home Experts of Indiana
    Carmel company started in 2021 by owner and lead inspector Shane Peterson, who has been inspecting since 2012 and has done 5,000+ inspections. A team of inspectors covers the metro, including multifamily and commercial buildings. Offers termite, radon, sewer scope, mold, well and septic add-ons. Full inspections start at $350.
  • HomeTeam of Indianapolis
    Locally owned office of the national franchise, run by owner and lead inspector Jeff Crane. Offers radon testing, sewer scopes and termite inspections as add-ons, plus commercial property inspections for investors.

Insurance Agents #

    Mass Ave agency owned by Jackson Blevins that focuses on landlords with small rental portfolios.
  • Merritt Hall Enterprises
    South side agency that writes landlord policies covering the dwelling, liability, tenant damage and lost rent during repairs.
  • Wexford Insurance
    Independent agency in Greenwood that shops several carriers for rental property coverage, including 1–4 unit homes, condos, short-term rentals and multi-property portfolios.

Lenders #

  • CPL Investments
    Indianapolis hard money lender offering fix-and-flip, short-term, and new construction loans up to 60–65% ARV. Works with newer investors and experienced developers.
  • Grandview Lending
    Family-owned mortgage broker on Keystone Crossing. Arranges investment property loans for short- and long-term holds and rehab loans based on ARV, with stated-income options for investors. Offices open seven days a week.
  • Indy Private Lending
    An individual private lender, not a broker or fund. Underwrites every loan personally, with approval in as little as a day. Lends 50–70% of as-is value (not ARV) at 10% and 2 points, from $10,000 to $1,000,000 with 6-month to 5-year terms. Investors only.
  • MM Lending
    Private lender since 2005 serving Indiana, Ohio, and Kentucky. Offers fix-and-flip and BRRRR loans up to 75% ARV, 1–4 unit ground-up construction, and DSCR rental loans. Covers up to 90% of purchase and 100% of rehab, with 7–10 day closings.
  • Union Savings Bank
    Cincinnati-based bank with a large Indianapolis branch. Offers investment property loans for 1–4 unit and multifamily rentals, plus blanket loans that combine several properties for cash-out refinances or new purchases.

Title Companies #

  • Eagle Land Title
    Small, owner-run title agency on the northwest side, led by Frank Banks. A longtime pick among local investors and brokers.
  • Investors Title Service
    North side title company that focuses on investor transactions, including back-to-back (double) closings and contract assignments.
  • Monument Title
    Title and closing company with offices on North College Ave, downtown and in Carmel. Home of Dan Bostick, a longtime favorite of Indy investors for assignments and double closings.

Utilities #

  • AES Indiana
    Electric utility for Indianapolis and parts of the surrounding counties. Start, stop and transfer service online. New account holders may have to pay a deposit unless their credit qualifies.
  • Citizens Energy Group
    Water, sewer and natural gas in Indianapolis, on one combined bill.
  • Indy Trash Pickup
    Look up the trash provider and pickup day for any Marion County address. Since 2026, Lakeshore Recycling Systems collects in most districts and city crews handle the rest. Curbside recycling is a paid subscription for now and is scheduled to become free countywide in 2028.
